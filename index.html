Primary Research Project

AESOP-RU: Syntactic Paraphrase Auditing Framework

MIPT & FRC CSC RAS (2024 – 2026) | Supervised by N.V. Chudova, D.A. Devyatkin

Framework

Designed and trained AESOP-RU, a ru-BART-large architecture fine-tuned with a custom masked-loss objective enforcing SynTagRus dependency-grammar constraints. Produces fine-grained syntactic paraphrases without altering lexical semantics across ~815k sentence pairs.
