Primary Research Project

AESOP-RU: Syntactic Paraphrase Auditing Framework

MIPT & FRC CSC RAS (2024 – 2026) | Supervised by N.V. Chudova, D.A. Devyatkin

Framework

Designed and trained AESOP-RU, a ru-BART-large architecture fine-tuned with a custom masked-loss objective enforcing SynTagRus dependency-grammar constraints. The system produces fine-grained, syntactically-controlled paraphrases without altering lexical semantics, built on an ~815k-pair corpus.

Key Research Finding: Audited seven frozen classifiers (RUBERT, mBERT, DistilmBERT, RoBERTa, ruGPT-3, GPT-2, OPT). Bidirectional encoders proved robust under syntax degradation, whereas causal autoregressive decoders displayed high sensitivity, showing that syntax causally influences decision boundaries in an architecture-dependent manner.
